FAQ

Questions About Social Media Management

What platforms do you manage?+
We manage Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, and X (formerly Twitter). Essential covers 2 platforms, Growth covers 3, and Scale covers 4. We'll recommend the best platforms for your business and audience during the strategy phase.
What types of content do you create?+
We create static graphic posts, Reels and short-form videos, Stories, and written captions. All content is brand-aligned, uses your visual identity and colors, and is designed to engage your specific audience and support your business goals.
Do you engage with our followers?+
Yes. All tiers include community engagement. Essential includes basic engagement and comment monitoring. Growth adds full community engagement. Scale adds advanced community management with comprehensive interaction handling across all platforms.
How do you develop our content strategy?+
We start with a discovery session to understand your brand, audience, competitors, and goals. We then build a content strategy defining themes, formats, messaging pillars, and a content calendar. Growth and Scale tiers include monthly strategy calls to continuously refine based on performance data.
Is paid advertising included?+
No. This service covers organic content creation, scheduling, and community management. Paid social media advertising (boosted posts, paid campaigns) and ad spend are not included. If you want to amplify reach with paid social, we can discuss that separately.
