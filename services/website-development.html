FAQ

Questions About Website Development

What is included in a website development project?+
All our website projects include custom responsive design, mobile optimization, contact and lead forms, basic SEO setup, speed optimization, and Google Analytics integration. Higher tiers add conversion-focused design, blog setup, booking integrations, e-commerce functionality, and more extensive copywriting.
How long does it take to build a website?+
A typical project takes 3-8 weeks from kickoff to launch depending on the tier and page count. Essential sites (up to 5 pages) typically take 3-4 weeks. Scale sites (up to 20 pages with advanced features) may take 6-8 weeks. We'll provide a specific timeline at the project kick-off.
Do you write the website copy?+
Yes. All tiers include professional copywriting — written to be both compelling for visitors and optimized for search engines. Essential includes basic copywriting, Growth includes up to 10 pages, and Scale covers up to 20 pages. Great copy is as important as great design, so we never skip it.
Will my website be mobile-friendly?+
Absolutely. All websites we build are fully responsive and mobile-optimized as a standard feature across every tier. With the majority of web traffic coming from mobile devices, this is non-negotiable in everything we build.
What happens after the website launches?+
All tiers include post-launch support: 14 days for Essential, 30 days for Growth, and 60 days for Scale. During this window we address bugs, make minor adjustments, and ensure everything is running smoothly. After that, we offer ongoing maintenance packages and can also support your site's growth through our Local SEO, Google Ads, and other services.
