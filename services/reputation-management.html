FAQ

Questions About Reputation Management

What is online reputation management?+
Online reputation management (ORM) is the practice of monitoring, influencing, and protecting how your business appears online. It involves generating positive reviews, responding professionally to existing reviews, monitoring brand mentions, and building long-term trust across Google, Yelp, Facebook, and other relevant platforms.
How does review generation work?+
Review generation involves creating systematic processes to encourage satisfied customers to share their experience publicly. This includes follow-up email and SMS campaigns, QR codes, review request templates, and strategic timing. The Growth and Scale tiers include active review request campaigns on top of the strategy framework.
Can you remove negative reviews?+
We cannot guarantee the removal of reviews, as that is ultimately controlled by the platforms. However, we can help flag reviews that violate platform policies, craft professional responses that mitigate damage, and build such a strong base of positive reviews that the impact of any negatives is significantly diluted.
Which platforms do you manage?+
Essential focuses on Google review management. Growth expands to Google plus major platforms including Yelp, Facebook, and industry-specific directories. Scale provides full multi-platform monitoring across all relevant channels where your business is reviewed online.
How quickly will I see results?+
Most businesses begin receiving new reviews within the first 30 days of our review generation campaigns going live. Building a consistently strong reputation profile typically takes 3-6 months, with compounding benefits becoming very significant over the longer term.
