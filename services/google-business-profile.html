FAQ

Questions About Google Business Profile

What is Google Business Profile management?+
GBP management involves optimizing and actively maintaining your business listing on Google Search and Maps. This includes keeping information accurate, posting regular updates and photos, responding to reviews, managing Q&A, and tracking local rankings — all to maximize visibility and attract more local customers.
How is GBP management different from Local SEO?+
GBP management focuses specifically on your Google Business Profile listing. Local SEO is broader — it includes GBP optimization plus on-page website optimization, citation building, content creation, and backlink development. Both work together for maximum local visibility, and many businesses benefit from both services.
What are Google Posts and why are they important?+
Google Posts are short updates that appear directly on your GBP in search results. Regular posting signals to Google that your business is active, which can positively influence local rankings, and provides direct value to people viewing your profile. All our tiers include consistent Google Post creation each month.
Will you respond to my Google reviews?+
Yes. All tiers include professional, brand-aligned review responses — up to 15/month for Essential, 40/month for Growth, and 75/month for Scale. Responding to reviews builds trust with potential customers and shows Google your business is actively engaged with its community.
How quickly can I see improvements to my Google ranking?+
Initial improvements often begin appearing within 30-60 days of optimization. Consistent improvement in local rankings is typically seen over 3-6 months as Google processes ongoing optimization signals, review accumulation, and content activity.
