FAQ

Questions About App Development

What types of apps does GrowthPoint Media build?+
We build custom mobile apps for iOS and Android as well as web applications. Our projects range from straightforward single-platform apps to complex multi-platform applications with advanced integrations, payment systems, and enterprise-level features.
How is the project scope and investment determined?+
App development investment is based on complexity, platform requirements, backend architecture, number of features, and third-party integrations. We use the three tiers as starting frameworks, with final scope and investment determined after a detailed discovery conversation about your specific requirements.
Do you help with App Store submission?+
Yes. App Store submission assistance is included in all tiers. We help prepare the necessary assets, metadata, descriptions, and technical requirements for submission to the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
Can you integrate the app with my existing systems?+
Yes. API integrations with existing business systems are a core capability. Basic integrations are included in Essential, advanced integrations in Growth, and complex enterprise integrations in Scale. We'll discuss your specific needs during discovery.
Do you provide ongoing maintenance after launch?+
All tiers include a post-launch support period (14-60 days depending on tier) for bug fixes and adjustments. Longer-term maintenance plans can be arranged separately, ensuring your app stays updated, secure, and running smoothly as your business grows.
