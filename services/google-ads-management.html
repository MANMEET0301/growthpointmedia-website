FAQ

Questions About Google Ads Management

What is included in Google Ads management?+
Our management covers everything needed to run successful campaigns: account setup, keyword research and strategy, campaign structure, ad copy creation, negative keyword management, bid and budget optimization, conversion tracking, and regular reporting. Higher tiers add A/B testing, remarketing, display campaigns, audience targeting, and more frequent optimization.
Is the ad spend included?+
No. The management fee covers our expertise and time managing your campaigns. Google Ads media spend is a separate cost paid directly to Google. We recommend businesses have a dedicated monthly ad budget to maximize results. We'll advise on appropriate budget levels during our strategy call.
How do you measure success?+
We set up comprehensive conversion tracking from the start so campaigns are measured against real business outcomes. Key metrics include cost per click, conversion rate, cost per acquisition, return on ad spend, and total lead/revenue contribution. You receive clear monthly reports with all performance data.
What is remarketing and is it included?+
Remarketing shows ads to people who have previously visited your website but didn't convert. These are highly effective because the audience has already shown interest. Remarketing campaigns are included in the Scale tier, along with advanced audience segmentation.
How soon will I see results?+
Google Ads can drive results quickly — often within the first week of campaigns going live. However, performance typically improves significantly over the first 1-3 months as we gather data, optimize bidding, refine targeting, and improve ad copy. The more data accumulated, the more efficiently your budget is allocated.
